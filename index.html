About

I'm a university instructor and coordinator in Management Information Systems, and a senior developer. My work sits where business and technology meet: understanding how an organization runs, then designing the database and the application that support it.

Over the years I've developed complete systems for retail, automotive, equipment servicing and subscription-based businesses, and I bring that practical experience into the classroom.
